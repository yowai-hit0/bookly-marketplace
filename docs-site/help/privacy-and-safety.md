# Your privacy and payment safety

## Your payment is handled by your provider, not by us

When you pay, you approve the charge on your own phone through your Mobile Money
provider. **Your card number, CVV and Mobile Money PIN never reach this site and
are never stored here.** We only ever learn that a payment succeeded, failed, or
is still pending — never your payment credentials.

The whole site is served over HTTPS, so traffic between your device and us is
encrypted.

## Your booking link is like a key

Your booking is reached through a private, unguessable link we email you — there
is no account and no password. Treat the link like a key:

- Anyone who has it can view and manage that one booking, so keep it to
  yourself.
- It can't be guessed, and it isn't tied to a web address anyone can walk
  through.
- If you ever think a link has been seen by someone else, ask the photographer
  to resend it — issuing a new link immediately stops the old one working.

## What we collect, and why

To arrange your shoot we collect only:

- your **name**, **email** and **phone number**, so we can confirm and reach you;
- the **shoot location**, **party size** and any **special requests** you give;
- the fact that you **consented** at booking, and when.

That's it. We don't build a profile, and we don't sell or share your details.

## Keeping and deleting your data

Your booking details are kept while they're needed to run and account for your
shoot. Rwanda's data-protection law (Law N° 058/2021) gives you the right to
have your personal data deleted.

To request deletion, **email the photographer**. Your personal details are then
removed from the booking records. One thing is deliberately kept even then: the
record that consent was given, because the law requires that consent remain
demonstrable — but it no longer carries your contact details.
