# Photographer guide

The admin side of Bookly, in plain terms. This round covers the two areas that
matter most day to day:

- **[Payments: fees, cash and refunds](./payments)** — how money comes in, how
  to record cash, how to refund, and what each payment state means.
- **[Security and your responsibilities](./security)** — your login, keeping
  the admin panel safe, and your data-protection duties.

More of the guide (calendar, catalogue, delivery) is planned; for anything not
covered here, the [developer docs](/developers/) describe how the system
behaves.
